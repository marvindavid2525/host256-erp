# Host256 ERP dashboard prototype

A responsive front-end prototype for a Uganda-focused business management suite. It brings the modules from the supplied Host256 ERP reference together in one dashboard:

- Point of sale
- Sales management
- Inventory and stock
- Accounting and finance
- People and HR
- Customer management
- Reports and analytics

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or external packages are required. Google Fonts and the typefaces load from Google Fonts when online; the dashboard itself works without them.

## Prototype interactions

- Navigate the modules from the sidebar.
- Search sample products and customers with the top search field (or `Ctrl/⌘ K`).
- Record a quick sale from the POS or dashboard. The sale appears in recent transactions and stock is reduced.
- Filter inventory by stock status and restock products.
- Export the sample sales transactions to CSV.
- Use the responsive menu on a small screen.

## Important

This is a static UI prototype, not a production accounting or point-of-sale system. Data is sample data held in the browser session; there is no authentication, backend, payment processing, tax calculation, or data persistence. Confirm local pricing, tax, receipts, and business processes before using a production system.
